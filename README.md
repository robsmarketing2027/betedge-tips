# BetEdge Tippek

Public, static copy of the BetEdge tips page: about 20 of the day's highest-probability sportsbook
selections, every sport, Hungarian UI, plus an **Eredmények** tab (`#eredmenyek`) that shows how every
earlier tip ended – hit rate against the expected rate, result at the reference odds, and the source of
each final score. Served by GitHub Pages at https://robsmarketing2027.github.io/betedge-tips/ and
refreshed whenever its owner starts the Claude routine "BetEdge tips: refresh (manual)", which follows [UPDATE.md](UPDATE.md): it settles
the earlier tips in `results.json`, then rewrites `tips.json`.

The page (`index.html`) and the brief are maintained in the private `betedge` repository under
`tips/`; copy them here when they change. No bookmaker is logged in to or scraped, no bet is placed,
and nothing here is a guarantee: every probability carries its source and date.
